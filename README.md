# PackSpec — Packaging Configuration App

PackSpec works out how a supplier should pack each part for a mixed-model assembly line:

- parts per box under the box rules (30 × 30 × 12 in, 30 lb),
- boxes needed in the gravity flow rack to cover a 4 h or 8 h target,
- a squared pallet on the standard 52 × 50 in footprint under 1,500 lb of parts and boxes.

It shows the loaded pallet in 3D, plan, front, and side views, draws the flow rack lanes, and produces a text specification to send to the supplier.

**Open the app:** https://olasubomiakanbi-tech.github.io/packspec/

The built-in test case uses invented data for demonstration.
